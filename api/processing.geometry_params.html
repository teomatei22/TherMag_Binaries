processing.geometry_params module¶

Geometry and model configuration for FEM post-processing from geometry.geo.

class processing.geometry_params.GeometryParams(geometry_type, variant='full', is_two_layer=False, T_inner=0.0, T_outer=0.0, L=None, R=None, W=None, H=None, R_sphere=None, R_cap=None, R_sph=None)[source]¶

Bases: object

Conductor dimensions and hollow/layer variant parsed from geometry.geo.

Inputs:

  • geometry_type – Family cylinder, prism, or sphere.

  • variant – Topology full, hollow, or layer_over_*.

  • is_two_layer – Whether an inner material layer is present.

  • T_inner – Inner-layer wall thickness [m].

  • T_outer – Outer-layer / hollow wall thickness [m].

  • L – Axial length (cylinder/prism) [m].

  • R – Cylinder radius [m].

  • W – Prism half-width [m].

  • H – Prism half-height [m].

  • R_sphere – Metal sphere radius [m].

  • R_cap – Sphere contact-cap radius [m].

  • R_sph – Optional far-field air-sphere radius [m].

Outputs:

GeometryParams – Dimension container for glyph scale and probe band layout.
